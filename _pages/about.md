I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Sydney, Australia, working with Dr. Chang Xu and Prof. Dacheng Tao.
I am also working as an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon, working with Dr. Vu Nguyen and Dr. Ajanthan Thalaiyasingam.
My current research interests lie in interpretable disentangled representation learning, tabular data, computer vision, and related deep learning applications.

I received my B.S. degree in Computer Science (top 2%) in 2018 from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, China.
I am also an invited reviewer for academic conferences ICML, ICLR, NeurIPS, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ACMMM, and journal IEEE TMM.
